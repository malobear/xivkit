Price Insight My pick

See item marketboard prices on hover!

Why it's on my list

Shows NQ/HQ marketboard prices and the most recent sale directly on an item's tooltip on hover, pulled from Universalis. In the default plugin repo

About Price Insight

Whenever you mouseover an item, it will show you the NQ/HQ marketboard prices at the bottom as well as the most recent purchase. Prices are retrieved from Universalis.app and cached for 90 minutes. To refresh a price you can tap Alt while hovering over an item.

Screenshots

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. In game, type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  3. Open All Plugins, search for Price Insight, and click Install.
