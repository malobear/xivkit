Progress & Checklists

  • XIV To-DoDuty completion tracker for dungeons, raids, and trials (less useful for the dailies checklist)

  • FFXIV CollectLook up and compare minions, mounts, and other collectibles you're tracking
  • Eureka TrackerEureka NM pop time tracker, the community standard
  • PAISSA DBTracks open housing plots and wards in real time, what I use to find and review options before bidding
  • Housing Plot ViewerBrowse screenshots and views for every plot so you can judge whether you'd like it without visiting in game
