Lifestream

A plugin to speed up Aethernet travel and World changing.

About Lifestream

- One click to teleport to any world or aethernet destination - Click on aetheryte shards inside zone map to teleport to them

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. Type /xlsettings and open the Experimental tab. Under Custom Plugin Repositories, paste this address, click the + button, then the save button.
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NightmareXIV/MyDalamudPlugins/main/pluginmaster.json
  3. Type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  4. Open All Plugins, search for Lifestream, and click Install.

Lifestream comes from the NightmareXIV repository, not Dalamud's official list. Dalamud's team has not reviewed it. Only add repositories you trust, and remove the repository again if you stop using its plugins.

Commands and tips

Almost everything in Lifestream runs through one chat command, /li, followed by where you want to go. The plugin's README doesn't list any of them, and the Help tab inside the plugin (open it with /lifestream) misses a few. Commands marked Not in the help aren't in that tab at all. Every command also works in a macro, so a hotbar button for /li fc or /li mb is one click from anywhere.

Worlds and data centers

/li
Go back to your home world. You can change what a bare /li does under Settings (open the world list, open the settings, or nothing).
/li <world>
Go to a world, on your data center or another one. The start of the name is enough, so /li bal is Balmung. Worlds on your current data center are checked first, so type enough letters to tell similar names apart.
/li <data center>
Go to a random world on that data center. Example: /li light
/li w
Open Lifestream's world travel window and pick a world from the list. /li world and /li travel do the same.
/li stop Not in the help
Cancel whatever Lifestream is doing, such as a trip that's stuck in a queue.

Data center travel is on by default. Lifestream leaves your party when it needs to; you can turn that and data center travel off in Settings.

Aetherytes and the aethernet

/li <aethernet shard>
In a city, go to an aethernet shard by part of its name. In Ul'dah, /li sapphire takes you to the Sapphire Avenue Exchange.
/li tp <aetheryte> Not in the help
Teleport to an aetheryte by part of its name, the same as picking it from the Teleport list. Example: /li tp tuliyollal
/li <world>, tp <aetheryte>
Go to another world, then teleport straight to an aetheryte there. Example: /li bal, tp kugane

You can also click an aethernet shard on the zone map to go there, without typing anything.

Market board

/li mb
Go to a market board.
/li <world> mb
Go to another world and straight to its market board, for checking or buying on a world you don't live on. Example: /li bal mb

Housing

/li home
Go to your own house. /li house and /li private do the same.
/li fc
Go to your Free Company's house.
/li ws
Go to your Free Company's workshop, all the way inside.
/li apt
Go to your apartment.
/li shared
Go to a house you share with someone else.
/li auto
Go to whichever of your homes you set as preferred in Settings (house, shared, FC or apartment).
/li <district> <ward> <plot>
Go to any plot on your current world. Districts are mist, lavender, goblet, shirogane and empyreum. Example: /li mist 5 12
/li <world> <district> <ward> <plot>
Go to a plot on another world, useful for venues and friends' houses. Example: /li bal goblet 3 30
/li <saved address> Not in the help
Give an Address Book entry an alias and it becomes its own command. Save a venue as venue and /li venue takes you there from anywhere.

For the house commands to walk you to the door, register your houses once in the plugin's House Registration tab (stand on the plot, then record the path to the entrance).

Grand Company and inns

/li gc
Go to your Grand Company's headquarters, for seal turn-ins and expert delivery.
/li gc <company>
Go to a specific Grand Company: mael, adder or flames. Example: /li gc flames
/li gcc
Go to the company chest in your Grand Company's city.
/li hc
Same as /li gc, but goes back to your home world first. /li hcc does the same for the company chest.
/li fcgc Not in the help
Go to your Free Company's Grand Company instead of your own, for FC seal turn-ins when the two differ.
/li inn Not in the help
Go to your inn room on the world you're on.
/li hinn Not in the help
Go back to your home world, then to your inn room.
/li inn <number> Not in the help
Go to a specific city's inn. Type a number that's too high, like /li inn 99, to see the list. Example: /li inn 2

Walking to the Grand Company counter uses the optional vnavmesh plugin. Without it Lifestream still teleports you to the right city.

Instances and special areas

/li <instance number> Not in the help
Switch to that instance of the zone you're in, when the zone has instances. Handy for hunts and FATEs. Example: /li 2
/li cosmic
Go to Cosmic Exploration, going back to your home world first if needed.
/li occult Not in the help
Go to the Occult Crescent.
/li island
Go to your Island Sanctuary.
/li firmament
Go to the Firmament in Ishgard.

Chaining and switching characters

/li <world> <shortcut> Not in the help
Most shortcuts above can follow a world name, and run once you arrive. /li bal gc goes to Balmung and then your Grand Company. Use a comma for anything longer: /li bal, venue.
/li <first> <last>@<world> Not in the help
Log out and back in as another of your characters on the same account. Add a world after it to travel there once logged in. Example: /li Jane Doe@Balmung

Build your own multi-step routes in the Custom Alias tab, and run them with /li plus the alias name.
