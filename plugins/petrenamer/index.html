P

Pet Nicknames

Give your minions, Carbuncles and Faeries cute nicknames and share them with your friends.

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. In game, type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  3. Open All Plugins, search for Pet Nicknames, and click Install.
