Doorbell

Play a chime when someone comes into a house.

About Doorbell

Play a chime when someone comes into a house, when they leave or when entering a house people are already inside. Icon by Flaticon

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. In game, type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  3. Open All Plugins, search for Doorbell, and click Install.
