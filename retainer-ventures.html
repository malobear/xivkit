Retainer ventures: what to send them for

Every venture your retainer can run, ranked by what it earns on the market board, with live prices and how fast the haul actually sells.

Field Exploration ventures are left out because their loot tables are random. Prices come from your own world, so pick it below.

Best ventures for your retainers

Each haul is priced at the lower of the current lowest listing and the recent average sale price, minus 5% market tax. Items that barely sell are filtered out, since a high price on something nobody buys is not real money.

New retainers copy the last one, so you only change what differs. Everything is saved in this browser.

      No ventures match. Try a lower minimum sales rate or a longer sell window.

      Sources and how this works

      • Venture list, item yields, and stat breakpoints: the RetainerTask sheets from XIVAPI, saved as ventures.json and refreshed after patches. A venture's yield steps up each time your retainer's gathering (or item level for combat classes) passes a breakpoint, so enter the stat shown on the retainer's own sheet.
      • Market prices and sales speed: pulled live from the Universalis API for the world you pick. Gil per hour assumes you resend the retainer the moment it returns.
      • Gil per hour is the rate while a retainer runs that venture. It cannot be kept up for items with few sales: a world only buys so many Lime Basil a day, however many retainers you send. The plan tab accounts for this by capping each item at a share of its daily sales, and shows gil per day instead.
