Neko Fans

Frustrated in a dungeon? Look at a cute cat girl!

About Neko Fans

Shows you an image of a random neko character. Click on the image to view the next one. You can choose different image sources, allowing you to look at Catboys, Waifus, Cats, Dogs and more. Supported APIs: nekos.best, Nekosia, NekosAPI, Nekos.life, Dog CEO, The Cat API, Pic.re, Purrbot and Waifu.im Warning: You might see naughty images

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. Type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  3. Open All Plugins, search for Neko Fans, and click Install.
