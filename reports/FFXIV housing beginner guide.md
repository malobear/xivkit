# Start small: a beginner's FFXIV housing roadmap

For a new small-house owner, the best starting pair is the Console Games Wiki "Player Housing" page for rules, limits and fixtures, plus the HGXIV Basics Guide for the decorating interface and layout workflow. No single official or community guide covers both exterior and interior, and no official Lodestone beginner guide turned up. There is also no "Personal Estate Permit" or "Free Company Estate Permit" item. The only permit is a size-matched **Construction Permit** bought at the Estate Hall after you win land (**450,000 gil** for a cottage), and the real variants are plot type (personal, Free Company, apartment) and FC-only Private Chambers. As of Patch 7.5 (April 28, 2026), a small house holds **300 indoor and 40 yard furnishings**, 6 vendors, 4 crafting facilities and 1 garden patch. The upkeep rule that matters is the **45-day inactivity demolition timer**, which resumed on October 26, 2026 after a security-related pause. Furnishing can start cheaply: Housing Merchant basics cost roughly 2,000 to 5,000 gil. Most useful plugins sit in the default Dalamud repo, but nearly all are hidden from browsing, and BDTH and Re:MakePlace are not default-repo plugins. All figures come mainly from community-edited wikis, with no primary-sourced Patch 7.5 notes, so they should be flagged for in-game verification. Housing is also slated for overhauls in Evercold (February 23, 2027).

## Two community resources beat the many dated SEO guides

The most complete reference is the Console Games Wiki "Player Housing" page. It covers the five districts, estate sizes, how to acquire land, customizing and demolition, and was last edited on October 21, 2026 ([Console Games Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)). For hands-on decorating, HGXIV is the strongest verified source. Its Basics Guide was written collectively by housing designers from its Discord and Twitter community. It covers the Housing menu, indoor furnishing, furnishing color, interior remodeling, preview, lighting adjustment, Layout Mode tools, counter-surface placement, grid snap and gamepad shortcuts. It does not cover exteriors ([HGXIV Basics Guide](https://hgxiv.com/basics-guide/)). The same site links a Glitch Guide and a lighting guide. It also links a Design Guide Google Doc labelled "Updated as of Patch 7.5X", which is the most patch-current beginner design resource found, though the doc itself was not opened ([HGXIV](https://hgxiv.com)). For small quality-of-life tricks such as placing from storage, an anonymous Carrd page collects tutorials, but it has no author or dates ([FFXIV Housing Tutorials](https://ffxiv-housing-tutorials.carrd.co/)).

Older articles are the trap. The PCGamesN and NerdBear buying guides date to the Patch 6.1 era and predate the lottery-only system and the 7.5 furnishing caps ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/final-fantasy-xiv-a-realm-reborn/ffxiv-housing-prices-locations-types), [NerdBear](https://nerdbear.com/how-to-buy-a-house-in-ffxiv-updated-for-6-1)). Even the Console Games Wiki is inconsistent. Its Storage page still lists the old 200/20 small-house caps. For inspiration, Square Enix's "Mister Foxclon's Neighborhood" recaps tour player-built houses, such as the December 2024 episode covering 11 designs ([Lodestone recap](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/blog/003684.html)). The earlier Episode 2 recap used data from April 2023, so some locations may have moved ([FFXIV Blog](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/blog/003464.html)).

Several sources could not be verified, and the site should not present them as vetted. Reddit, Eorzea Collection, Icy Veins and Gamer Escape all blocked automated fetches or yielded nothing. The decorating subreddit appears to be r/FFXIVhomeandgarden, seen only through mirrors, so link it only after a manual check. No individual YouTube beginner video was verified either, though the HGXIV channel is linked from its site.

## One permit, three plot types, and strict ownership limits

The word "permit" is a trap for newcomers. After winning a plot, you buy a Construction Permit at the Estate Hall (Social menu, Housing, Estate Hall). It costs **450,000 gil (cottage), 1,000,000 (house) or 3,000,000 (mansion)**. You have **45 days** to build or the land is reclaimed, and the plot size fixes the house size ([Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)). Since Patch 6.1 all land is sold by lottery on a 9-day cycle (5 days of entry, 4 of results). You get one entry per period, and the full plot price is deposited and cannot be withdrawn. Winners who fail to finalize lose 50% of the deposit, and losers are refunded in full within 90 days ([Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing), [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)). Small plots cost **3,000,000 to 3,750,000 gil** depending on grade, versus 16 to 20 million for medium and 40 to 50 million for large. Price does not vary by district ([Console Games Wiki: Residential District](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Residential_District)).

The actual variants lie in who owns the plot. Personal buyers need a level 50 combat class and Second Lieutenant rank in a Grand Company. FC buyers need FC rank 6, at least 4 members, and 30 days of membership for the purchaser ([Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)). A player can hold one personal house and, if they lead an FC, one FC house. Personal and FC plots cannot be converted into each other, so you must relinquish and repurchase ([Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)). Apartments sit outside that limit: one per character, **500,000 gil**, up to 90 tenants per building, with no auto-demolition ([Console Games Wiki: Apartment](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Apartment)). Apartments top out at 150 indoor furnishings, 4 vendors and 2 flowerpots, with no garden patches or yard. A personal estate can be shared with up to 3 friends, and FC houses alone offer Private Chambers at 300,000 gil per member ([Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)). The wiki words the one-house rule inconsistently, so the site should say to confirm in game. Island Sanctuary is a separate system that the notes did not cover.

Tool-wise, "permit" also appears in vendor hiring. The cheapest Player Housing Vendor permit is the 3,000 gil Steward Permit for an Estate Manservant or Maidservant. Others cost event currencies ([Player Housing Vendors](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing_Vendors)).

## Limits, upkeep and the live demolition timer

Patch 7.5 raised indoor caps by 50% and doubled outdoor caps ([WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more), [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)). The notes' sources were third-party and wiki articles that agree with each other, not Lodestone patch notes.

| Estate | Indoor (before to now) | Yard (before to now) | Vendors | Garden patches |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apartment / Private Chamber | 100 to 150 | none | 4 | none (2 flowerpots) |
| Small (Cottage) | 200 to 300 | 20 to 40 | 6 | 1 (up to 8 beds) |
| Medium (House) | 300 to 450 | 30 to 60 | 8 | 2 |
| Large (Mansion) | 400 to 600 | 40 to 80 | 10 | 3 |

Patch 7.5 also capped rendered furnishings at 400 on screen and added a Display Limits setting. A visual bug hid some indoor furnishings after the increase, and Square Enix gave no fix timeline; whether it was fixed is unverified ([MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/final-fantasy-xiv-housing-bug-is-hiding-some-indoor-furnishings-after-the-item-cap-increase)). Fixtures are straightforward: four mandatory exterior pieces (roof, wall, windows, door), four optional ones (roof decoration, wall decoration, placard, fence), and interior wall, flooring and ceiling light ([Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)). Placed furnishings can no longer be traded, and a Storeroom (Social menu, Housing) holds items that cannot return to inventory, with capacity matching the furnishing cap ([Console Games Wiki: Storage](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Storage)). Garden plants stop growing in storage.

Upkeep is one rule: if the owner (or, for FC houses, any FC member) does not enter the estate for **45 days**, it is demolished. Warnings arrive at 30 days in game and at 35 and 42 days by email. For shared estates, only the owner's visit counts. After demolition, 80% of the plot price can be recovered from the caretaker within 35 days, but permits and exteriors are not refunded ([Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)). The site must handle a stale wiki note carefully. The wiki says demolition is "currently paused" as of October 21, 2026, but the official Lodestone notice says timers resumed October 26, 2026 at 1:00 a.m. PDT ([Lodestone notice](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/news/detail/ff0334468a9e32da6ee45ff0a44b2b181117a23e), [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/final-fantasy/final-fantasy-14-lets-players-back-into-the-mmo-after-php-vulnerability-fears-locked-them-out-for-nearly-24-hours-but-that-means-housing-demolition-is-back-on-the-cards/)). Treat the timer as live. The notice does not say whether timers were reset. Relocation (since 4.1) discounts the new plot by about 15% of the old plot's value, but its transfer limits predate 7.5.

## Furnishing cheaply with merchants, previews and a handful of tools

Housing Merchants are the cheapest route. Interior walls, flooring and ceiling lamps are mostly 5,000 gil, chairs and tables about 2,000, bookshelves and rugs about 5,000, and floor lamps 3,000 ([Housing Merchant list](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Template:Housing_Merchant)). That puts a furnished small house plausibly well under 1,000,000 gil, which is an inference, since one older source's "under 1M" claim is unverified. Other routes include crafting, garden plants, Island Sanctuary, seasonal events, Feast rewards and Mog Station items. Vendor, retainer, Feast, seasonal and Mog Station items cannot be previewed before purchase, but merchant items can ([Housing Items](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing_Items), [HGXIV Basics Guide](https://hgxiv.com/basics-guide/)). Useful in-game habits from the HGXIV guide include rotating while holding an item, grid snap, counter-surface placement, Storage Mode, and "Move to Front Door" if stuck. Which furniture is cheapest to craft, plus Gold Saucer and achievement furniture, were not verified.

For websites, Universalis gives crowd-sourced market prices and Garland Tools builds crafting lists ([Universalis](https://universalis.app/about), [Garland Tools](https://www.garlandtools.org/db/)). Housing Snap offers searchable player screenshots filtered by house size and district ([Housing Snap](https://housingsnap.com/)). PaissaDB tracks plots for sale per world, but its redirected URL returned a 404, so re-check the link before publishing. Patch-specific furniture lists exist from [Siliconera for 7.1](https://www.siliconera.com/all-new-furnishings-in-ffxiv-7-1/) and [GGRecon for 6.5](https://www.ggrecon.com/guides/ffxiv-housing-items-patch-6-5/).

On plugins, the master list checked November 15, 2026 shows that these have real default-repo entries and need **no `experimental` tag**: Housingway, Housemate, PaissaHouse, Housing Herald, Target Furniture, Better Housing, Housing Cull Fix, Facade, Doorbell, Allagan Tools, Item Search and Price Insight ([PluginMaster](https://kamori.goats.dev/Plugin/PluginMaster)). Nearly all are flagged `IsHide: true` (489 of 507 entries), so the page must tell users to search the exact name. Housingway adds indoor lighting and camera tweaks ([GitHub](https://github.com/Abyeon/Housingway)). Housemate shows fixture and object info for inspiration but does not assist placement ([GitHub](https://github.com/perchbirdd/Housemate)). PaissaHouse alerts on new plots on your home world ([GitHub](https://github.com/zhudotexe/FFXIV_PaissaHouse)). Target Furniture selects overlapping items ([GitHub](https://github.com/InitialDet/TargetFurniture)). Housing Cull Fix removes the 400-item display limit ([GitHub](https://github.com/Abyeon/HousingCullFix)). Better Housing is the only non-hidden entry, but it targets Dalamud API 9 and was last updated December 2023, so recommend the others instead ([GitHub](https://github.com/Sevii77/properhousing)). BDTH, a custom-repo plugin that lets players exceed placement limits, is absent from the master list and would need `experimental`, with only dated sources found. Re:MakePlace is a free standalone Windows app (about 2 GB) for planning layouts, not a Dalamud plugin, and should be listed as an external app ([Re:MakePlace](https://remakeplace.itch.io/remakeplace)). No official Square Enix policy on housing tools was found, so a standard third-party disclaimer is advisable. That is a judgment, not an official statement, and 2026 saw tools such as Mare Synchronos shut down under legal pressure ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/news/after-mare-synchronos-shut-down-whats-next-for-ffxiv/)).

## What may change in Evercold

Housing rules are still moving. Patch 7.0 added Estate Holder Rights, letting an owner bar a visitor for 10 days, and reclassified wards on some worlds ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/7-0-ffxiv-housing-changes-include-estate-holder-rights-ward-reclassifications/)). Patch 7.1 added interior designs. Yoshida reportedly confirmed two housing overhauls for the Evercold window: a UI overhaul aimed at casual players, and a second he did not name ([games.gg](https://games.gg/news/ffxiv-housing-overhaul-wow-comparison/)). Speculation that the second is plot availability is just that. WowCarry reports that applying a Large interior to smaller plots is in development ([WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more)). Evercold releases February 23, 2027, with early access February 19 ([Lodestone news](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/news/)). The site should not promise new plots or limits.

## Conclusion

The housing section should be organized around corrections to common misconceptions: there is one real permit, ownership is capped at one personal and one FC house plus an apartment, and the demolition timer is live again. Because the best community references disagree on details (old caps, a stale "paused" note), the section should date-stamp every number and link to the wiki for verification rather than restate it as settled fact.

Priorities for the build are clear. First, verify in game or on Lodestone the 7.5 caps, relocation limits, and the Estate Hall and entry-permission settings that the notes could not source. Second, check the unverified links (Reddit, PaissaDB, Icy Veins, Eorzea Collection) manually before including them. Third, plan a refresh after Evercold, since the announced overhauls could change the limits and interface this report describes.
