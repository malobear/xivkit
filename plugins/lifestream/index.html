Lifestream

A plugin to speed up Aethernet travel and World changing.

About Lifestream

  • One click to teleport to any world or aethernet destination
  • Click on aetheryte shards inside zone map to teleport to them

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. Type /xlsettings and open the Experimental tab. Under Custom Plugin Repositories, paste this address, click the + button, then the save button.
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NightmareXIV/MyDalamudPlugins/main/pluginmaster.json
  3. Type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  4. Open All Plugins, search for Lifestream, and click Install.

Lifestream comes from the NightmareXIV repository, not Dalamud's official list. Dalamud's team has not reviewed it. Only add repositories you trust, and remove the repository again if you stop using its plugins.

Commands and tips

Almost everything in Lifestream runs through one chat command, /li, followed by where you want to go. Its README doesn't list any of them. The full list is in the plugin's Help tab (type /lifestream), copied below as it appears in game.

Also works (not in the help)

/li stop
Cancel whatever Lifestream is doing, such as a trip stuck in a queue.
/li inn
Go to your inn room on the world you're on. /li hinn goes back to your home world first.
/li inn <number>
Go to a specific city's inn. A number that's too high, like /li inn 99, prints the list. Example: /li inn 2
/li <instance number>
Switch to that instance of the zone you're in, when the zone has instances. Handy for hunts and FATEs. Example: /li 2
/li fcgc
Go to your Free Company's Grand Company instead of your own, for FC seal turn-ins when the two differ.
/li tp <aetheryte>
Teleport to an aetheryte by part of its name, without changing world. Example: /li tp tuliyollal
/li <world> <shortcut>
Put a shortcut after a world name and it runs once you arrive. Use a comma for anything longer, like an alias: /li bal, venue. Example: /li bal gc
/li <alias>
Give an Address Book entry or a Custom Alias route an alias and it becomes its own command. Example: /li venue
/li <first> <last>@<world>
Log out and back in as another of your characters on the same account. Example: /li Jane Doe@Balmung

Tips

  • Names can be shortened. /li bal is Balmung and /li sapphire is Ul'dah's Sapphire Avenue Exchange. Worlds on your current data center are checked first.
  • Register your houses once in the House Registration tab (stand on the plot, then record the path to the door) so the estate commands walk you inside.
  • Install the optional vnavmesh plugin for the Grand Company commands to walk you to the counter. Without it they still teleport you to the right city.
  • Everything works in a macro, so a hotbar button for /li fc or /li mb is one click from anywhere.
  • Settings decide what a bare /li does (go home, open the world list, open settings, or nothing), whether data center travel is allowed, and whether to leave your party first.
  • You can also click an aethernet shard on the zone map to go there without typing anything.

The extra commands and tips are XIVKit's notes, worked out from the plugin's code. Commands can change when the plugin updates.
