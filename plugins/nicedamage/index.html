About NICE Damage Flyouts

Adds the message NICE to your damage flyout when you get a hit ending in 69. Also 67, optionally. Also, your own custom numbers now.

How to install

  1. Install XIVLauncher with Dalamud and start the game through it.
  2. Type /xlplugins to open the plugin installer.
  3. Open All Plugins, search for NICE Damage Flyouts, and click Install.
