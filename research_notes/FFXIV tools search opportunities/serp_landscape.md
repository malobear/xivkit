# FFXIV SERP landscape and gap opportunities (as of 2026-11-11)

Method note: about 20 searches were run through the agent's web search tool (a US-only engine, not a live Google page with the result order visible). The result lists below are a proxy for who ranks. They are not a verified Google top-10 and do not show whether an AI Overview or featured snippet appeared. Quality judgments are labelled "(assessment)". Game context: the current patch is the 7.5x series (7.55 shipped around August 2026 and 7.56 is mentioned). The 8.0 expansion *Evercold* was moved from January 2027 to **February 23, 2027, with early access on February 19** ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/news/final-fantasy-xiv-evercold-release-date-announced/); [ConsoleGamesWiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Evercold); the original January window is in [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/04/final-fantasy-xiv-evercold-expansion-switch-2-version-and-evangelion-collaboration-announced)). The months before launch are a period when "how to prepare" and "what to finish" content has high value. The site already has `get-ready-for-evercold.html`.

The user's site currently covers retainer ventures, trade-in mounts, Hunt for Astronomy, an MSQ tracker, inventory decluttering, jump-puzzle macros, Fan Fest bingo, a Dalamud plugin directory, a watchlist, and an Evercold prep page (from the repo's root `*.html` list).

## Who are the dominant players and what do they own?

### Takeaway
Two sources show up on almost every English FFXIV help query. **Icy Veins** owns current-content guides (Cosmic Exploration, Occult Crescent, relics, gil, retainers, Island Sanctuary). **Console Games Wiki (CGW)** owns reference and system pages. Behind them, a long tail of news-guide sites (Sportskeeda, Twinfinite, Destructoid, Prima, Escapist, TheGamer, Siliconera, PCGamesN, Dot Esports) fills in, often with stale patch-dated pages. Gamer Escape did not show up in any sampled query. Japanese queries are owned by Game8.jp. German queries are served mostly by old mein-mmo.de articles.

### Inferences
- (assessment) Icy Veins is the main "current content" competitor and updates quickly. Head terms for brand-new content (e.g. Occult Crescent, Cosmic Exploration) are hard to win. Its weak spots are older system pages (BLU 6.45, the stale Field Operation overview).
- (assessment) CGW is authoritative but structurally fragmented. Task-oriented queries ("what should I buy", "what's left for me to do") land users on reference tables, not answers.
- (assessment) Gamer Escape's absence from every sampled result suggests it has lost ranking share to CGW. This was not verified on Google.
- (assessment) The database tools (Garland, Teamcraft, Collect, Saddlebag) win navigational and tool queries but rarely rank for natural-language "how to" queries. A site that wraps tool-like interactivity in a guide page can target the space between them.

## Patterns of gaps and concrete opportunities

### Takeaway
The best opportunities are interactive, filterable, localized (EN/JA/DE/FR) tools for **stateful or "what's left" intents** that wikis answer only with long tables and news sites answer with decaying articles. The Evercold launch on February 23, 2027 is a timing window for prep and checklist content.

### Cited Findings
- Fragmentation: Island Sanctuary returned 9 separate CGW sub-pages plus third-party tools for a single intent. — [CGW Island Workshop](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Island_Workshop), [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/island-sanctuary-workshop-guide)
- Staleness: the CGW weekly checklist dates to 7.05, Blue Mage pages are 6.45-era, the fashion report had nothing newer than 2024, and Icy Veins' Field Operation overview is outdated. — [CGW Timers](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Timers), [IV BLU](https://icy-veins.com/ffxiv/blue-mage-pve-dps-spell-summary), [Siliconera FR](https://www.siliconera.com/?p=1023080), [IV FO](https://icy-veins.com/ffxiv/occult-crescent-field-operation)
- Beatable thin/commercial content: account-seller and spam pages rank for "gil making 2026" and "leveling 1-100". — [AccountShark](https://accountshark.net/blog/ffxiv-leveling-guide-1-100), [internationaltrucks spam](https://presentationbuilder.internationaltrucks.com/final-fantasy-14-levelling-guide)
- Weak non-English coverage: no complete current German mount list was found, and Japanese mount results are concentrated in Game8.jp. — [mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/die-neuen-reittiere-begleiter-final-fantasy-xiv-42/), [Game8.jp](https://game8.jp/ff14/285468)
- Patch-volatile caps: tomestone caps changed in 7.4 (Mathematics uncapped) and 7.56 (memory-crystal cap 450 to 900), which makes static answers stale. — [finalboss.io](https://finalboss.io/ffxiv-7-4-is-nuking-aesthetics-tomes-math), [playnews.gg](https://www.playnews.gg/en/guides/final-fantasy-xiv-7-56-unleashes-the-sadistic-heavyweight-cca-echo-at-12-unlimited-coffer-and-30-memory-crystals-per-match)
- A MassivelyOP column (June 2026) speculates that Evercold adds a new weekly activity system, so weekly-checklist demand may grow at 8.0. This is speculation, not confirmed. — [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2026/06/22/wisdom-of-nym-speculating-on-final-fantasy-xiv-evercolds-weekly-activity-system/)
- AI Overviews and click-through: one study-based report says the top result's CTR falls 58% versus expected when an AI Overview is shown, and that AIO keywords are overwhelmingly informational. One analysis found 73% of sites lost organic traffic (median -15.8%), while sites tied to community, events, or transactions held up better. These are SEO-vendor sources, not gaming-specific data. — [Stan Ventures](https://www.stanventures.com/news/google-ai-overviews-slash-website-traffic-new-data-shows-6819.md.txt), [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/the-ai-search-reckoning-is-dismantling-open-web-traffic-and-publishers-may-never-recover), [custodydesign Jan 2026](https://custodydesign.com/blog/state-of-google-search-january-2026/)

### Inferences (concrete opportunity list, assessment)
1. **Currency "what to buy" planners**: bicolor gemstones (all vendors by expansion), tomestones of poetics/mathematics, wolf marks, allied/centurio seals, Cosmic credits, Occult Crescent currencies. Each would be filterable by type (mount, minion, housing, orchestrion, glamour), show cost, value, and icons, and be localized. Current SERPs are fragmented, contradictory, and expansion-sliced. This extends the user's existing trade-in-mounts pattern.
2. **Interactive weekly/daily checklist with reset countdown** (localStorage tick-offs, region-aware reset times). The incumbent is a 7.05-dated wiki page plus Endwalker-era news. Refresh it for Evercold's possible new weekly system.
3. **"What's left" collection planners** (mounts/minions by source, filterable, in JA/DE/FR). German has no complete list. Game8.jp's Japanese list is partly 2021-dated. FFXIV Collect is the reference but ranks poorly for "how to get X". A per-item "how to get" landing page set could capture long-tail queries.
4. **Blue Mage spell checklist** (filter by duty/level/unlocked, current spell count). Every ranking page is stale.
5. **Relic progress calculators** (Phantom relic tomestones/steps remaining, Cosmic Tools). Icy Veins writes the guides but has no tracker.
6. **Evercold prep hub** (what to finish before February 23, 2027: limited-time content, relic finish, currency dumps before a new tier, bicolor spend-down). A time-limited window with news sites as the main competition.
7. **Avoid**: pure-fact FAQ pages ("when is weekly reset", "tomestone cap") and head terms for new content (Icy Veins/CGW). The first group is AIO-prone and the second is strongly owned.
8. **Weekly fashion report**: a gap, but it needs weekly upkeep and its source of truth is social (Kaiyoko on Reddit/X). Low priority unless it can be automated.

### Gaps
- No data on actual search volumes. Keyword-tool numbers are needed to rank these opportunities.
- AI Overview presence was not observed per query. The CTR impact numbers are generic and not FFXIV-specific.
- French SERPs and the quality of JA/DE results beyond mounts were not sampled.
- YouTube's share of results (likely large for jump puzzles, leveling, and gil) was not measured.
