Theorycrafting & Calculators

  • The BalanceJob theorycrafting hub, rotation guides, discord
  • EtroGearset planner and BiS builder
  • Ariyala's ToolboxAlternate gearset/glamour planner

Guides & Reference

  • Gamer Escape WikiGeneral item, quest, and game info. Alternate to the Console Games Wiki
  • HeavenswhereInteractive maps for hunts, gathering nodes, aether currents, and treasure (currently showing a maintenance page, recheck before relying on it)

Progress & Checklists

  • FFXIV Relic TrackerTracks relic weapon progress and what's still needed
  • Sightseeing Log HelperHelper for the in-game Sightseeing Log bingo
  • Ocean Fishing ScheduleUpcoming ocean fishing voyage schedule, blue fish, and achievement info
  • Fish TrackerPredicts weather/time windows for catching specific fish, the community standard for fishing spreadsheets

Plugins

  • AetherphoneAdds a full smartphone interface in-game, social feed, messaging, photo sharing, market board, games. Recreates a lot of a home-screen experience. Custom repo, not in the default plugin directory
  • A Realm ReorganizedScans the Glamour Dresser, Armoire, bags, Armory Chest, Saddlebag, and retainers for duplicates, partial sets, and items now eligible for the Armoire, then highlights them right in those windows. In the default plugin repo but hidden from browsing. Still in the author's test channel, haven't tried it myself yet

macOS Tools

  • PatinaFXApple silicon-only shader tool that loads ReShade community shaders into any Metal-rendered game, trying as an alternative after XIV on Mac's built-in ReShade setup turned out unstable
