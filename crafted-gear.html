Item level 770 crafted set: Courtly Lover

Crafted gear shopping list

Pick a job and see the cheapest HQ market board listing for every piece of its current crafted gear, live from Universalis: Courtly Lover for combat jobs, and Crested armor with Gold Thumb's tools for crafters and gatherers. Each job's full set total shows on its button, so you can see which ones are cheap to gear right now.

Updated Nov 20, 2026

Courtly Lover Item Level 770
      • Prices are the lowest single listing on any world in the market you pick, so a full set usually means a few world visits. The 5% market tax is on top.
      • Prices are for HQ only. HQ and NQ share the same item level, but HQ adds roughly 10% more main stat plus extra substats, and nearly everything crafters list is HQ anyway, so NQ is rarely cheaper. Melding materia is up to you; listings that already have materia are counted too, if they happen to be cheapest.
      • Rings are not unique, so the two ring slots use the two cheapest ring listings.
      • The combat body piece also comes as an "Ornate" version. It has the same base stats but five guaranteed materia slots instead of two, so you can meld it fully without risking overmelds. It is not crafted (earlier sets' Ornate pieces came from Wondrous Tails), so it is rare and usually costs far more. The page checks both and shows whichever is cheaper.
      • Jobs that wear the same armor set only need a new weapon: tanks share Fending, healers share Healing, magical ranged share Casting, and physical ranged share Aiming. Melee splits three ways: Maiming for Dragoon and Reaper, Striking for Monk and Samurai, and Scouting for Ninja and Viper. Accessories are shared more widely: Slaying covers Monk, Dragoon, Samurai, and Reaper, and Aiming covers physical ranged plus Ninja and Viper.
      • Crafters and gatherers have no tradeable gear at the newest item level, so their list uses the best crafted gear you can buy: the item level 750 Crested set and Gold Thumb's tools. Every crafter wears the same Crested armor and every gatherer another, so a second crafter or gatherer only needs new tools.

      Sources

      • Item list, item and job names in all four languages, and icons come from XIVAPI, saved as crafted-gear.json and refreshed when a new crafted set comes out.
      • Market prices are pulled live from the Universalis API each time you load the page or press Refresh. Universalis only knows what players with its uploader have seen, so a listing can be gone by the time you get there. The time under each price says when that item's data was last updated.
