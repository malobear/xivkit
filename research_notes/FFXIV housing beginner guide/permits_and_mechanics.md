# FFXIV housing permits, plot sizes, and core mechanics (as of 2026-11-15)

Research note: this covers a limited set of sources. Most facts come from the community Console Games Wiki (player-edited), plus Lodestone and news outlets. The Lodestone patch-note pages for 7.5 itself were not fetchable (404 or 403), so the 7.5 numbers rest on third-party articles and the wiki. They agree with each other.

## What permits exist, how are they obtained, and how many houses can one player own?

### Takeaway
There is no "Personal Estate Permit" or "Free Company Estate Permit" item in the sources. The only permit is a size-specific Construction Permit, which you buy from the Estate Hall after winning land. The real distinctions are plot type (personal, FC, apartment) plus FC-only Private Chambers. One player can hold one personal house, one FC house (via an FC they lead), and one apartment. Island Sanctuary is a separate system, not covered by the housing sources.

### Cited Findings
- Land is bought first, then you must buy a Construction Permit from the Estate Hall (Social menu > Housing > Estate Hall > Purchase Construction Permit). It costs 450,000 gil (cottage), 1,000,000 (house), 3,000,000 (mansion). You have 45 days after buying land to build, or the land is reclaimed. The plot size locks the house size. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)
- Since Patch 6.1 all land is sold by lottery, not first-come-first-served. Each lottery runs on a 9-day cycle: 5 days of entry, 4 days of results. — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- Lottery rules: one entry per period. The full plot price is deposited on entry and entries can't be withdrawn. Winners must finalize during the results period or forfeit the plot and lose 50% of the deposit. Losers get a full refund, claimable within 90 days. Entries must be made on your home world. — [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- Personal buyer requirements: a level 50 Disciple of War or Magic class and Second Lieutenant rank in a Grand Company. — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- FC buyer requirements: FC rank 6 or higher, at least 4 members, and 30 days of membership for the purchasing player. — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- Ownership limits: a player and an FC can each hold one house. To hold two, you must lead an FC and buy one personal house and one FC house. Apartments and private chambers don't count toward this limit. A personal plot can't be converted to an FC plot or the reverse. You must relinquish and repurchase. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)
- Apartments: one per character, can be held alongside an estate. Get it from the Apartment Caretaker in an apartment lobby. Cost is 500,000 gil. Requires level 50 on a combat class and Second Lieutenant rank. Up to 90 tenants per building. Estate sharing is not allowed. Apartments are not subject to auto-demolition. — [Console Games Wiki: Apartment](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Apartment)
- Shared estate: a personal estate can be shared with up to 3 friends as tenants. Tenants can use the stable and garden or move and place furniture. — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- Private Chambers exist only in FC houses. Each costs 300,000 gil per member, one per member (512-player FC cap). They are not refunded on demolition. Personal housing has none. — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- Grandfathered accounts with multiple plots must reduce to one personal and one FC house before entering the lottery. — [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)

### Inferences
- For a beginner explainer, "permit" should be described as the Construction Permit that matches the plot size, not as separate personal or FC permits. Anyone with a small house already presumably bought a 450,000 gil permit.
- The Console Games Wiki says apartments are "not shared" and are separate from the one-house limit, so a small-house owner can also hold one apartment.

### Gaps
- The Island Sanctuary is not covered by these sources. It is a separate system, not tied to housing wards or lottery, and needs its own check.
- No source confirmed an item literally named "Personal Estate Permit" or "Free Company Estate Permit". The Market Board is not mentioned as a permit source. Permits are Estate Hall purchases.
- Wiki wording is inconsistent on the one-house rule ("one each" vs "one private and one FC plot per world per service account"). Verify in-game before writing it as fact.
- The wiki houses all of this on community-edited pages, not official Lodestone pages.

## Plot sizes, furnishing limits, wards, districts, and prices

### Takeaway
Five districts (Mist, Lavender Beds, Goblet, Shirogane, Empyreum), 30 wards each, 60 plots per ward. Plot price depends on size and plot grade, not district. As of Patch 7.5 (April 28, 2026), furnishing caps are Small 300 indoor / 40 outdoor, Medium 450 / 60, Large 600 / 80.

### Cited Findings
- Districts: Lavender Beds (Gridania), Mist (Limsa Lominsa), Goblet (Ul'dah), Shirogane (Kugane; added 4.0, plots from 4.1), Empyreum (Ishgard; added 6.0, plots from 6.1). Each has 30 wards; each ward has 60 plots (30 primary, 30 subdivision); 1,800 plots per district. — [Console Games Wiki: Residential District](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Residential_District)
- Per server: 9,000 plots (900 Large, 2,100 Medium, 6,000 Small) and 27,000 apartments. Wards went from 24 to 30 in Patch 6.3. — [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- Plot prices by grade (the pages list only the cheapest and most expensive grades): Small 3,000,000 (fifth class) to 3,750,000 (first class). Medium 16,000,000 to 20,000,000. Large 40,000,000 to 50,000,000. The wiki says prices are not broken down per district and that there is no difference between wards except appearance. Costs were unified across worlds in 3.3. — [Console Games Wiki: Residential District](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Residential_District)
- Furnishing limits before 7.5 versus after, verified by two independent summaries:
  - Apartment/Private Chambers: 100 to 150 indoor, no outdoor.
  - Small (Cottage): 200 to 300 indoor, 20 to 40 outdoor.
  - Medium (House): 300 to 450 indoor, 30 to 60 outdoor.
  - Large (Mansion): 400 to 600 indoor, 40 to 80 outdoor.
  - Indoor up 50%, outdoor doubled.
  - Sources: [WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more) (third-party) and [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing). [Icy Veins via search results] also say indoor +50% and outdoor x2 (page itself returned 403).
- Per-size garden patches: Small 1 patch (up to 8 beds), Medium 2 (16), Large 3 (24). Gardening was added in 2.2. Apartments cannot have garden patches (but up to 2 flowerpots). — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing); [Apartment](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Apartment)
- Housing vendor (NPC) limits: 6 for a cottage, 8 for a house, 10 for a mansion, 4 for an apartment. — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- Ward classification for some worlds (effective June 29, 2024): wards 1-6 FC only, 7-20 FC or private, 21-24 private only, 25 FC only, 26-29 FC or private, 30 private only. This applies only to certain worlds (Dynamis worlds like Cuchulainn, Golem, Halicarnassus, Kraken, Maduin, Marilith, Rafflesia, Seraph; EU worlds Chaos' Phantom, Sagittarius, Light's Alpha, Raiden). Other worlds' classification varies by ward. — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/7-0-ffxiv-housing-changes-include-estate-holder-rights-ward-reclassifications/)

### Inferences
- A small-house owner's per-size limits are 300 indoor and 40 outdoor now. The old 200/20 figures still appear on some wiki pages (the Console Games Wiki Storage page still lists 200/20, 300/30, 400/40), so any guide should flag that older sources are outdated.
- The "Fifth class to First class" price ranges are plot grades that appear to track ward/plot position, but the wiki doesn't say how grades map to districts.

### Gaps
- Exact per-district price ranges: not found. The sources say price depends on size and grade only.
- Which exact grade each ward/plot has: not found.
- I did not get official Lodestone 7.5 patch notes, so the numbers are not primary-sourced.
- A known visual bug after the cap increase can hide some indoor furnishings (not lost). Square Enix said a fix was being worked on, with no timeline. — [MMOHuts](https://mmohuts.com/news/final-fantasy-xiv-housing-bug-is-hiding-some-indoor-furnishings-after-the-item-cap-increase). I did not verify whether it has since been fixed.

## Upkeep, demolition, relocation, and current lottery or ward status

### Takeaway
An estate is demolished after 45 days without a visit by the owner (personal) or any FC member (FC). The timer stops when you enter. Auto-demolition was paused in October 2026 due to a PHP security issue and resumed October 26, 2026. Lottery status could not be confirmed as anything but the standard 9-day cycle.

### Cited Findings
- 45 days without access triggers demolition. Warnings arrive at 30 days (in game), 35 days and 42 days (email). Entering the estate stops the timer. Unbuilt plots have the same timer. For shared estates, only the owner's visit counts, not a tenant's. — [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing); [Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- After demolition you can recover 80% of the plot price from the resident caretaker within 35 days. Permits, exteriors, private chambers and some furnishings are not refunded. — [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- Apartments are not subject to auto-demolition. — [Console Games Wiki: Apartment](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Apartment)
- Relocation (since 4.1): the new plot is discounted about 15% of the old plot's value. You can transfer up to 40 outdoor and 400 indoor items (the wiki's relocation figures predate 7.5, so they may have changed). Personal and FC plots can't be directly converted. — [Console Games Wiki: Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- October 2026 pause: Square Enix suspended logins over PHP flaws and paused auto-demolition timers. The official Lodestone notice says the timer resumes October 26, 2026 at 1:00 a.m. PDT. — [Lodestone notice, Oct. 22](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/news/detail/ff0334468a9e32da6ee45ff0a44b2b181117a23e); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/final-fantasy/final-fantasy-14-lets-players-back-into-the-mmo-after-php-vulnerability-fears-locked-them-out-for-nearly-24-hours-but-that-means-housing-demolition-is-back-on-the-cards/)
- Past pauses (not current): NA after Hurricane Helene, LA wildfires in early 2025, and a European pause that ended with Patch 7.3. — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/ffxiv-european-automatic-housing-demolition-resumes-in-7-3/)
- Lodestone news page checked November 15, 2026 showed no housing, demolition or lottery notices in the latest items. It confirmed Evercold release on February 23, 2027 (early access February 19). — [Lodestone news (NA)](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/news/)

### Inferences
- The Console Games Wiki pages still say demolition is "currently paused" (note dated October 21, 2026). That note is stale. Based on the Lodestone notice it resumed October 26, 2026. A guide should tell players the 45-day timer is live.
- Since the timer resumed only about three weeks ago, anyone who was inactive may still be mid-countdown. Whether Square Enix reset timers on resume is not stated in the notice (it said nothing on grace periods).

### Gaps
- The "current state" of the lottery (which wards are open, per-world availability) is per world and in game only. Not found online.
- Whether the October 2026 pause reset or extended timers: not stated.
- Exact current relocation transfer limits post-7.5: not verified.

## Basic mechanics: Estate Hall, housing menu, fixtures, permissions, storage, gardens, stables

### Takeaway
Most mechanics are well covered by the wiki, but "Layout Mode" and the entry-permission settings were not documented in fetched sources. Storage, fixtures, gardens, chocobo stables and vendor limits were confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Exterior fixtures: four required (roof, exterior wall, windows, door) and four optional (roof decoration, wall decoration, placard, fence). Interior: wall, flooring, ceiling light. Patch 7.1 added Interior Designs (changing pillars, moldings, stairs); 7.5 added the Dark Minimalist Style set. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House); [WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more)
- Yard furnishings: Move, Rotate, Remove options. Placed yard furnishings can no longer be traded. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)
- Storeroom: holds housing items that otherwise can't return to inventory. Indoor and outdoor separate, found under the Storeroom tab of Housing (Social menu). "Storage mode" in layout mode moves furnishings straight to the storeroom. Garden patches and flowerpots in storage don't grow. Miniature Aetherytes and Chocobo Stables can't be stored. Storeroom capacity matches the furnishing cap. — [Console Games Wiki: Storage](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Storage) (the numbers on this page are pre-7.5)
- Entry status on the map: yellow house = locked, blue = public; teleport view red = locked or empty, green = open. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)
- Chocobo Stable: every estate size has one. Apartments have a shared stable outside the lobby. In FC houses, members can feed stabled chocobos with Krakka Root. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)
- Estate Holder Rights (Dawntrail 7.0): FC Master, private owner or secondary rightsholder can add an unwanted visitor to a Barred Character List for 10 days. — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/7-0-ffxiv-housing-changes-include-estate-holder-rights-ward-reclassifications/)
- Housing merchants listed on the wiki include Junkmonger, Material Supplier, Estate Maidservant, Estate Manservant (location coordinates incomplete). — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing)
- 7.5 display changes: max 400 furnishings rendered on screen at once; a new Display Limits setting in System Configuration reduces other characters drawn in housing areas. — [WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more)
- Apartments can use Flowerpots (max 2), Orchestrion, Armoire, Crystal Bell, Summoning Bells and NPC vendors (cap 4), with crafting facilities capped at 2. — [Console Games Wiki: Apartment](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Apartment)

### Inferences
- Beginner explainer ordering: win lottery, buy permit within 45 days, choose exterior fixtures, then furnish and use the storeroom.

### Gaps
- How to enter housing mode and Layout Mode controls, Estate Hall service list (beyond the permit), and the in-game entry permission settings (the specific Allow/Deny options) were not found in fetched pages. They are standard game features but I did not source them. Do not state specifics without checking.
- Housing chest and wardrobe/Armoire details were not retrieved.
- Estate Hall services such as the relocation process and the Housing Merchant list need an in-game or Gamer Escape check.

## Recent patches (7.x) and upcoming changes through Evercold

### Takeaway
7.1 added interior designs, 7.0 added Estate Holder Rights and ward reclassification, 7.5 (April 28, 2026) raised furnishing caps and added a display cap. Two housing overhauls were reported as targeting Evercold (release February 23, 2027): a UI overhaul and an unnamed one.

### Cited Findings
- Patch 7.0/Dawntrail: Estate Holder Rights and ward reclassification (June 29, 2024). — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/7-0-ffxiv-housing-changes-include-estate-holder-rights-ward-reclassifications/)
- Patch 7.1: interior designs. — [Console Games Wiki: House](https://consolegameswiki.com/wiki/House)
- Patch 7.3: ended the European demolition pause. — [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/ffxiv-european-automatic-housing-demolition-resumes-in-7-3/)
- Patch 7.5 "Trail to the Heavens" released April 28, 2026: furnishing caps up (see above), 400-item display cap, Display Limits option, Dark Minimalist Style. — [WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more)
- Yoshida reportedly confirmed two housing overhauls at the Berlin Fan Festival: one a housing UI overhaul to make elaborate housing easier for casual players, and a second he did not name ("probably the number one thing on the list a lot of people are requesting"). Both target the Evercold window. Community speculation is that the second is plot availability, but this is only speculation. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/ffxiv-housing-overhaul-wow-comparison/)
- WowCarry says Yoshida discussed applying a Large house interior to smaller plots; it is in development and not in 7.5. Not announced: freeform placement or furniture scaling. — [WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/news/ffxiv-update-75-exciting-new-housing-features-and-more)
- Evercold release: Tuesday, February 23, 2027 (early access February 19). — [Lodestone news (NA)](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/news/)
- Search results mention 7.4 "Into the Mist" as already released. I did not fetch 7.4 housing changes.

### Inferences
- Because the Evercold overhauls aren't specified, a beginner explainer should not promise new plots or changed limits and should say mechanics may change in 2027.
- Treat the "Large interior on small plots" idea as a possible future feature only.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve 7.4 housing changes or any 7.5x/7.6 notes (if those patches exist).
- No Evercold housing details from an official source (Live Letter or Lodestone). The games.gg and WowCarry articles are secondary and the games.gg article was not directly confirmed against a Lodestone or Letter from the Producer transcript.
- Whether new wards/districts are coming: no source found.
