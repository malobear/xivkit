# Best beginner FFXIV housing guides for a new small-house owner (as of 2026-11-15)

## What are the best beginner housing guides, and how current are they?

### Takeaway
The strongest verified picks are HGXIV (Basics Guide, Design Guide, lighting guide) for hands-on decorating, and the FFXIV Console Games Wiki "Player Housing" page for rules, limits and fixtures. Current patch per Lodestone is 7.58 (scheduled Nov 10, 2026); Evercold is due Feb 23, 2027. Many "how to buy a house" articles are SEO guides from 2022-2023 and partly outdated. Several sources I wanted (Reddit, Eorzea Collection, Icy Veins, Gamer Escape) could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Current patch: Patch 7.58 scheduled Nov 10, 2026; Evercold pre-orders opened Nov 4 with Feb 23, 2027 release — [Lodestone news](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/lodestone/news/)
- Console Games Wiki "Player Housing" is the most complete reference: districts (Lavender Beds, Mist, Goblet, Shirogane, Empyreum; 30 wards each), estate sizes, acquiring (lottery, requirements, permits), customizing, demolition/reclamation. Last edited 21 Oct 2026, so very current. Cites patches through 7.1 (interior designs) and 7.5 (furniture limits increased) — [Console Games Wiki: Player Housing](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Player_Housing) (the fetched page was /wiki/Housing, which the search results show redirecting to the same article; fetched URL: https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- Wiki limits table for Small (Cottage): 300 indoor / 40 yard furnishings, 6 vendors, 4 crafting facilities, 1 garden patch. Note the wiki says 7.5 increased furniture limits, so verify these numbers in-game; I could not confirm whether the table was updated for 7.5 — [Console Games Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- Exterior has four mandatory fixtures (roof, wall, windows, door) and four optional (roof decoration, wall decoration, placard, fence). Interior fixtures: ceiling light, flooring, interior wall. Placed furnishings can no longer be traded. Furnishing Preview exists since 5.0 — [Console Games Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- Wiki notes automatic demolition was paused on all data centers as of Oct 21, 2026 (PHP security vulnerability/login outage). Temporary; flag for time-sensitive copy — [Console Games Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing)
- HGXIV Basics Guide: collective work by housing designers from the HGXIV Discord/Twitter (authors listed: Ashen Bride, Dividus Yliaster, Synt Slap). Covers the Housing menu (Social menu), Indoor Furnishing, Furnishing Color, Remodel Interior, Preview, Adjust Lighting, Layout Mode tools, Counter Surface Placement, Grid Snap, gamepad shortcuts. Does NOT cover exterior; no patch/date refs on the page (copyright 2024); limits only briefly — [HGXIV Basics Guide](https://hgxiv.com/basics-guide/)
- HGXIV site: Basics Guide, Glitch Guide (tricks for interior/exterior decorating), Design Guide (Google Doc, "Updated as of Patch 7.5X"), lighting guide (Google Doc). Run by the HGXIV Podcast team; YouTube, Twitch, Twitter, Discord linked — [HGXIV](https://hgxiv.com), [About](https://hgxiv.com/about/)
- Beginner placement tricks (floating furniture on PC, place-from-storage keybind, storing items with attachments): carrd page, author not named (links to a Twitter and a Reddit profile), no dates — [FFXIV Housing Tutorials](https://ffxiv-housing-tutorials.carrd.co/)
- Icy Veins "Guide to Obtaining and Decorating a House" appears in search results and a search summary called it among the more current general guides (cites level 50 + Second Lieutenant requirement); direct fetch returned 403, so content and date are unverified — [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/housing-guide)
- Getting-a-house basics from search summaries (secondary, check against the wiki): one plot per character, lottery is the only sale method, 45-day inactivity demolition, small-plot permit ~450,000 gil, apartments 500,000 gil. Plot prices vary by source — [Console Games Wiki](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Housing), [Video Games SI lottery explainer](https://videogames.si.com/guides/ffxiv-housing-lottery-explained)
- Official blog "Mister Foxclon's Neighborhood Ep. 2 Recap" (June 22, 2023): 10 player-built house tours with locations, data as of April 28, 2023, so some may have moved. Useful as official inspiration, dated — [FFXIV Blog](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/blog/003464.html)
- Outdated/older: PCGamesN housing article (patch 6.1 era) and NerdBear (updated for 6.1) are old — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/final-fantasy-xiv-a-realm-reborn/ffxiv-housing-prices-locations-types), [NerdBear](https://nerdbear.com/how-to-buy-a-house-in-ffxiv-updated-for-6-1)

### Inferences
- There is no single verified "start here" guide that covers both exterior and interior. Best link pair: Console Games Wiki Player Housing (rules, fixtures, limits, exterior) plus HGXIV Basics Guide (UI/layout mode, interior workflow).
- HGXIV's Design Guide, labelled 7.5X, is the most patch-current beginner design resource found, though the page itself is only a Google Doc link I did not open.
