# Build calculators where wikis only publish tables

XIVKit's best search opportunities are small, data-driven tools for questions players type every week but that no high-authority page answers interactively: a **leveling/EXP calculator**, a **Bicolor Gemstone and voucher "grind, buy, or sell" page** (which absorbs the repo's minion, orchestrion and barding ideas), **reset countdown plus weekly checklist**, a **Mini Cactpot and Wondrous Tails helper**, an **"old gear, materia and dye" extension of the inventory page**, and, before Evercold ships, an **Occult Crescent Phantom Job checklist** and a **"spend before Evercold" currency planner**. Two of those three things work against nearly every one of these ideas until they are fixed: the JA/DE/FR translations cannot be indexed (client-side only, no hreflang, no per-language URLs), and the site's most time-sensitive page, `get-ready-for-evercold.html`, is in neither `sitemap.xml` nor `llms.txt`. Measured CLS of 0.34 is the third site-wide issue. The window that matters is the run-up to Evercold (8.0): pre-orders opened November 4, 2026, the benchmark lands in early December, early access starts **February 19, 2027** and launch is **February 23, 2027** ([MMORPG.com](https://www.mmorpg.com/features/all-the-details-from-ffxivs-fan-fest-tokyo-keynote-2000139232)). Every demand score here is an estimate. No keyword-volume tool, Search Console data or rendered Google SERP was available. Demand comes from Google autocomplete presence, the number of publishers covering a topic, and recurring forum questions. Ownership comes from a US search index that approximates Google but does not reproduce its order. Treat the ranking as a prioritized hypothesis to validate with Search Console once the indexing fixes ship.

## Three site-level fixes gate every idea below

**Fix 1: make the translations indexable.** `i18n.js` chooses the language from `?lang=`, then localStorage, then the browser, and rewrites text client-side. No page carries hreflang, `sitemap.xml` lists no language URLs, and `index.html`'s canonical points every variant at `https://xivkit.com/` (repo: `i18n.js`, `sitemap.xml`, `index.html` line 17). Googlebot therefore sees only English. Google's guidance is to give each language its own URL and connect them with hreflang, not to switch content by cookie or browser setting ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)). Until per-language URLs (`/de/...` or at least crawlable `?lang=de` with self-referencing canonicals), hreflang clusters and sitemap entries exist, the localization work has no search value. Two smaller localization gaps compound this. The guides carry only 3 to 8 `data-i18n` attributes each, so their body copy is English, and the README says item names stay English in every language. That gives up the clearest non-English advantage, which is official in-game item names that machine-translated competitors get wrong ([games.gg DE](https://games.gg/de/news/final-fantasy-14-patch-755-abschluss-von-dawntrail-mit-relics-und-hildibrand/), [mmogah DE](https://www.mmogah.com/de/news/ffxiv/2026-efficient-gil-making-methods-in-ffxiv-no-crafting-required)). XIVAPI sheets should return names per language, but the exact `language` parameter on beta.xivapi.com was not verified.

**Fix 2: index the Evercold page.** A direct check confirms `get-ready-for-evercold.html` appears zero times in `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt`. The page has no canonical tag and no `data-i18n` attributes. One researcher worried that it still showed January. It does not: its meta description, lede and countdown already say February 19 and February 23, 2027, so that concern is resolved. Indexing is the remaining gap, and it matters most for this page because demand for it peaks within the next 14 weeks.

**Fix 3: fix CLS and image weight.** The November 8 PageSpeed report records **CLS 0.34**, the only failing Core Web Vital, along with oversized icons and render-blocking CSS (repo: `pagespeed-report-2026-11-08.md`; `guide-ideas.md` lines 347-362). Every page shares this cost, so fix it before adding pages.

A fourth, cheaper habit: stamp titles and H1s with the year or patch. Autocomplete keeps appending "2026", "this week" and patch numbers to help queries ([autocomplete: how to make gil](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ffxiv+how+to+make+gil), [autocomplete: returning](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ffxiv+returning)), and players still search legacy names like "beast tribe" and "poetics" ([autocomplete: beast tribe](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ffxiv+beast+tribe)), so titles should name both the old and new terms.

## Tools beat articles where answers go stale or need math

Across about 20 sampled queries, the results fell into three patterns. **Icy Veins** owns current-content head terms such as Cosmic Exploration, Occult Crescent and relics, and updates quickly ([Icy Veins Cosmic Exploration](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/cosmic-exploration)). **Console Games Wiki** owns reference pages but splits its content into many sub-pages (Island Sanctuary alone returned 9) and sometimes ranks with `MobileDiff` and template URLs ([CGW template](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Template:Cosmic_Exploration_nav)). Recurring and "what should I buy" queries are fragmented or stale. The weekly checklist leader is a wiki page last flagged for patch 7.05 ([CGW Timers](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/Timers)). Bicolor Gemstone results contradict each other on voucher value ([Thonky](https://www.thonky.com/final-fantasy-xiv/list-of-bicolor-gemstone-items), [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/ff/news/how-to-get-and-use-bicolor-gemstone-vouchers-in-final-fantasy-xiv)). Blue Mage pages are still titled for 6.45 ([Icy Veins BLU](https://icy-veins.com/ffxiv/blue-mage-pve-dps-spell-summary)). Gil sellers and boosting services rank for "gil making 2026", "leveling 1-100" and Evercold prep ([AccountShark](https://accountshark.net/blog/ffxiv-gil-making-guide-2026), [MMOGah](https://www.mmogah.com/news/ffxiv/what-you-should-do-before-ffxiv-80-evercold), [WowCarry](https://wowcarry.com/blog/final-fantasy-xiv/ffxiv-evercold-release-date-early-access-guide)), which signals that authority sites have not claimed those queries.

Database tools such as Teamcraft, Garland, FFXIV Collect and Saddlebag win navigational queries but rarely rank for natural-language "how do I" phrasing. That leaves room for guide pages built around a working tool, which is already XIVKit's pattern. AI Overviews add a structural argument for tools. SEO-vendor studies (not specific to games) report top-result CTR dropping 58% when an Overview appears, and find that pages built around transactions or community held up better than purely informational ones ([Stan Ventures](https://www.stanventures.com/news/google-ai-overviews-slash-website-traffic-new-data-shows-6819.md.txt), [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/publishers/the-ai-search-reckoning-is-dismantling-open-web-traffic-and-publishers-may-never-recover)). A one-line fact like "when is weekly reset" will be answered in the Overview. A countdown with a personal checklist will not.

Players phrase help queries with a small set of templates: "how to unlock X", "what to do with X", "where to spend / what to buy [currency]", "[currency] to gil", "what level does [job] start at", "best way to level X", and "X tracker / calculator / this week" ([autocomplete: what to do with](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ffxiv+what+to+do+with), [autocomplete: calculator](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=us&q=ffxiv+calculator)). The "to gil" and "worth it" phrasings are value judgments that live market prices answer better than prose, and XIVKit's Universalis-backed pages already do this.

## Evercold's February 23 launch sets the build order

The calendar between now and launch is quiet for game content and busy for search. Pre-orders opened November 4. The 8.0 benchmark and character creator arrive in early December. Early access starts February 19 and launch is February 23, 2027 ([MMORPG.com](https://www.mmorpg.com/features/all-the-details-from-ffxivs-fan-fest-tokyo-keynote-2000139232)). Seasonal events follow a regular cycle: Starlight in mid-December (2025's began December 17), Heavensturn around December 31, Valentione's about January 29 to February 10, and Little Ladies' Day from about February 10 ([senseos.gg](https://senseos.gg/guides/final-fantasy-xiv/ffxiv-seasonal-events/), [Lodestone Starlight 2025](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/pr/blog/002910.html)). Several plausible ideas face mature, maintained competition or need data a static site cannot get. **Fish and timed-node trackers** compete with ff14fish (updated 2026-11-03) and Fieldcraft ("Verified for Patch 7.58") plus at least two more trackers ([ff14fish](https://ff14fish.carbuncleplushy.com/), [Fieldcraft](https://ffxiv-fieldcraft.com/)). **Collection and achievement trackers** compete with FFXIV Collect and Lalachievements ([FFXIV Collect](https://ffxivcollect.com/)). **Crafting solvers and gear planners** compete with Teamcraft, whose repo was pushed 2026-11-10 ([GitHub API](https://api.github.com/repos/ffxiv-teamcraft/ffxiv-teamcraft)). BiS (The Balance, Etro) and glamour databases (Eorzea Collection) are named from general knowledge and were not verified in this research. **Live Occult Crescent FATE/CE pop trackers** need crowd-reported data, and three or more already exist ([ffxiv-occult](https://ffxiv-occult.com/), [xivstats](https://tracker.xivstats.com/)). **Island Sanctuary planners** serve fading Endwalker content ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/island-sanctuary-workshop-guide)). **Head terms for current content** (Cosmic Exploration guide, Occult Crescent guide) belong to Icy Veins. **Patch-day news, Fan Fest recaps and English per-event walkthroughs** belong to Sportskeeda, MMORPG.com and the news sites ([Sportskeeda Fan Fest](https://www.sportskeeda.com/mmo/final-fantasy-xiv-fan-fest-tokyo-2026-all-major-announcements-updating-live)). **One-fact FAQ pages** ("when is weekly reset", "tomestone cap") are the queries most likely to be answered inside an AI Overview. A **weekly fashion report** has no current owner on the web, but its source of truth is Kaiyoko on social media and it needs weekly upkeep, so skip it unless it can be automated. **Japanese prose guides** face Game8.jp, which held 8 of 10 results for the mount query ([Game8.jp](https://game8.jp/ff14/285468)). Spend Japanese effort on tools, with titles that follow the formulaic "〇〇 入手方法 / 一覧 / 金策" patterns. The **generic new-player FAQ** is owned by six or more high-authority guides (row 26). The repo's own exclusion list still applies: Triple Triad cards, Doman Mahjong, MGP items broadly, materia melding, housing lottery explainers, Deep Dungeon mechanics, Ocean Fishing scoring, job unlock order, beast tribe reputation, weekly tomestone cap and augmented gear, mirroring plugin READMEs, and Free Company vs static (`guide-ideas.md` 366-410). Aethertek (#27) should wait for the automation-content policy decision (`guide-ideas.md` 312-318).

## Facts the researchers could not reconcile

Several game facts conflict across sources, and any page that states them should hedge or verify against the Lodestone first. **Early access eligibility:** most outlets say all pre-orders qualify, but Siliconera suggests only some editions do ([Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/final-fantasy-xiv-expansion-evercold-release-date-falls-in-february/), [Final Weapon](https://finalweapon.net/2026/10/30/final-fantasy-xiv-evercold-expansion-release-date-early-access-pre-orders/)). The leveling calculator should not hard-code 110. **Tomestones at 8.0:** the conversion-at-reduced-rate pattern is precedent only ([Prima](https://primagames.com/tips/ffxiv-how-to-exchange-your-old-tomestones)), and XIVKit's own Evercold page cites Icy Veins on a "tomestone replacement" ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/ffxiv/evercold-progression-changes)). Method limits apply to every ranking above. Autocomplete was US English only, and JA/DE/FR phrasings were not sampled. Reddit returned 403 errors, so no thread counts or upvotes are available. French SERPs were not sampled. Gamer Escape, Game8 English, The Balance and YouTube were not profiled. No Search Console data shows which XIVKit pages already rank.

## Conclusion

XIVKit's advantage is not content volume. It is that its pages compute an answer: live market value, a personal checklist, a shareable state. The ranking shows that this is exactly where the SERP is weakest. Wikis publish tables, news sites publish articles that age out within one patch, and gil sellers fill the gaps with thin copy. The highest-return work is therefore unglamorous. Make the existing localization and Evercold page visible to Google, then ship calculators for the "how long", "what's it worth" and "what's left" questions, with titles stamped by patch. The localization fix is the largest unpriced bet. German and French have no current native mount or currency references, and data-driven pages carrying official item names would face almost no competition there. That only pays off once each language has its own indexable URL.

Evercold resets several competitive positions at once. Every weekly checklist, leveling page and currency guide goes stale on February 23. A site that has tools ready, and can regenerate them from game data within days of each patch, can win queries that Icy Veins and CGW currently hold. The first step after shipping the fixes is to connect Search Console and replace these estimated demand scores with real impression data before committing to the second wave.
