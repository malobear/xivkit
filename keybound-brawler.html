IV
Masterstroke IVMagitype Magister

Title for 100 clears. Losses don’t count, so it’s 100 runs at a perfect clear rate.

  • 100% clear rate100 runs · 13h 20m
  • 80% clear rate125 runs · 16h 40m
  • 60% clear rate167 runs · 22h 16m

By clear #16 you can already afford every reward. The rest is for the title.

  • Never buy the Keybound CofferIt drops free with every clear. Skip the 910 MGC price tag.
  • Spend before 10,000 MGCCredits cap at 10k. The title grind earns ~35k, so shop as you go.
  • Barrier HP = scoreEach wave pays a bonus for barrier HP left. Prioritize magic pots for the score buff.
  • Queue as a partyUp to 4 can register together. Undersized runs scale enemy HP down.

Costs & rewards: FFXIV Console Games Wiki (Oct 29, 2026). Run times are estimates. Keyboard required · Gold Saucer, W’zazanh.
