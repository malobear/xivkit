# Housing utilities research (as of Nov 2026, patch 7.5x)

Data in `utilities.json`. Icons all verified as PNG via XIVAPI asset endpoint (HTTP 200, image/png).

## Items that exist (all in JSON)
- Estate "facilities" (every size, one each): Chocobo Stable (7989, 125,000 gil, Housing Merchant), Miniature Aetheryte (6600, 14,470 GC Seals, Second Lieutenant), Moogle Letter Box (6475) / Regal Letter Box (7115) = the housing mailbox, Summoning Bell (7064), Crystal Bell (13061, Aesthetician), Orchestrion (14045, plus tabletop versions 17954, 35555, 21870), The Unending Journey (13069), Armoire (8789).
- Bell/Box/Orchestrion/Armoire/Journey items are crafted and sold on the Market Board; no vendor price found, so prices left blank.
- Mannequin (23846, 10,000 gil).
- Vendor permits (3,000 or 5,000 gil from Independent Sutlers or apartment Apartment Merchants): Mender 7156, Materia Melder 16704, Steward 13055, Junkmonger 7154, Material Supplier 7153, Journeyman Salvager 23230 (the housing Calamity Salvager). Vendor cap 4/6/8/10 by size.
- Crafting facilities (7065-7072), cap 2/4/6/8, 30 min CP buff for crafters level 90 or below.

## Dropped / do not exist
- "Mini Aetheryte": real name is Miniature Aetheryte.
- "Mailbox": real name Moogle Letter Box / Regal Letter Box.
- Retainer Vocate: city NPC only, not a housing item (Summoning Bell replaces it).
- Calamity Salvager: housing version is the Journeyman Salvager (permit).
- Wall-mounted Mannequin, Personal Chamber item, Item Search, Fisher/Gatherer tools, Dinner Table facility: no such housing item found. Private Chambers are FC rooms (300,000 gil each, Second Lieutenant, level 50), not a furnishing.
- Glamour Dresser cannot be placed in any estate. Aquariums and Display Stand are decorative only.

## Uncertain
- Mannequin cap wording ("facilities" in text, but numbers equal vendor cap).
- Chocobo Stable variants cosmetic (inferred).
- No patch 7.5x-specific housing utility changes were checked; wiki pages used may lag the live game. Stellar benches (48307+) are Cosmic Exploration items, not housing facilities.
